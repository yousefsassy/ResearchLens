## Relevance policy requiring approval

- **Full:** directly answers the whole question at its requested level of detail. An independently sufficient passage is a valid alternative, even if the draft cites a different page.
- **Partial, positive:** explicitly supplies a substantive complementary answer facet (such as all assumptions, the applicability limitation, the unfiltered construction, or the named dataset pair). This does not mean the chunk alone is a complete answer.
- **Partial, not positive:** incomplete fragments, illustrative examples, missing numeric mappings, broader background, or a neighboring variant that does not supply a complete requested facet.
- **Irrelevant:** no direct evidence for the requested answer. This is question-relative, not a judgment of scientific quality. Mere keyword overlap, performance tables and bibliography entries are not evidence.
- Explicit paper restrictions are honored. For questions whose wording does not mandate a particular PDF, accurate secondary-source alternatives are proposed (Q005/Q009/Q013). The original review describes the set as source-specific, so these are an explicit scope decision for the human reviewer.
- Judge the actual chunk, not all information visible on its PDF page. Do not add a chunk because neighboring text or an unextracted image contains the answer. Do not automatically exclude noisy chunks when their evidence is still readable.
- Apply the same policy to every system. These candidate decisions were made after seeing diagnostics/rankings, so the audit is not blinded. A separate reviewer should judge evidence before looking at system identities or rank changes.

**Metric limitation:** the existing schema and evaluator use a flat binary list. They cannot express alternative evidence groups, graded relevance, or whether all answer facets were covered. Adding equivalent answers increases the Recall@k denominator; it need not increase the score. Metadata preserves grades, inclusion decisions and reasoning, but the evaluator ignores them. Approve the binary policy before treating this as benchmark ground truth. No metric or script change is proposed here.
